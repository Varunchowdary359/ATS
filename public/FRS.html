MODULE 1 Headcount & Budget Governance

Job Requisition Module (4-Stage Approval Chain)

Governs how hiring demand is originated, justified, budget-cleared, and authorized by senior leadership.

1.1

The 4 Sequential Approval Stages

Stage 1: Originator Step 1

Hiring Manager (Submitted)

Action: Identifies talent need, fills target title, headcount count, budget range, and business justification, then submits the requisition.

Stage 2: Level 1 Sign-off Step 2

Department Head (HOD)

Action: Validates team workload, verifies replacement vs new headcount expansion, checks seniority, and signs off Level 1 approval.

Stage 3: Level 2 Budget Clearance Step 3

Finance Controller

Action: Cross-checks requested salary band against sanctioned corporate budgets and assigns the official Cost Center / Client project code.

Stage 4: Final Authorization Step 4

HR / Talent Acquisition Lead

Action: Grants final approval, stamps the permanent Requisition ID (e.g. REQ-2026-0001), and assigns the lead recruiter.

1.2

Requisition UI Role Mapping

1. Hiring Manager: Creates requisitions, defines requirements & salary targets, and tracks approval progress.
Stage: Creator (HM)
2. Department Head: Reviews headcount alignment, team capacity needs, and signs off Level 1 management approval.
Stage: L1 Sign-off
3. Finance Approver: Performs financial audit, verifies compensation bands, assigns Cost Center / GL codes, and clears Level 2 budget.
Stage: L2 Budget Clearance
4. Recruiter / HR: Conducts final HR sign-off (L3), assigns lead recruiters, and unlocks JD drafting action.
Stage: L3 & Sourcing
1.3

Step-by-Step Requisition Approval Flow

1
Hiring Manager Creates & Submits Requisition Actor: Hiring Manager

Enters Job Title, Number of Openings, Department, Salary Band, and Justification. Clicks Submit. Requisition state moves to Pending L1 (Department Head).

2
Department Head Review & Approval (L1) Actor: Department Head

Reviews team capacity. Clicks Approve (Department Head). Requisition state moves to Pending L2 (Finance Controller).

3
Finance Controller Budget Clearance (L2) Actor: Finance Approver

Verifies compensation band against departmental budget, maps Cost Center code, and clicks Approve (Finance). State moves to Pending L3 (HR Lead).

4
HR Lead Final Authorization & ID Lock (L3) Actor: HR / TA Lead

Approves sourcing feasibility and assigns lead recruiter. Requisition moves to APPROVED with permanent ID locked (REQ-2026-0001).

1. HM Submits ➔ 2. HOD Approves ➔ 3. Finance Approves ➔ 4. HR Lead Approves ➔ Requisition Approved (REQ-XXXX)
MODULE 2 Content, Calibration & Publishing

Job Description (JD) Module (3-Stage Governance & Publishing)

Governs how approved requisitions are transformed into calibrated Job Descriptions, reviewed, and published across candidate sourcing channels.

2.1

The 3 Sequential JD Governance Stages

Stage 1: Author Step 1

Recruiter / TA Specialist

Action: Selects the Mandatory Linked Requisition, drafts responsibilities, requirements, and salary band via AI or Enterprise Templates, then submits for review.

Stage 2: Technical Review Step 2

Hiring Manager

Action: Validates technical role mission, primary daily duties, mandatory skill matrix weights, and approves technical calibration.

Stage 3: Compliance Clearance Step 3

HR & Compliance Lead

Action: Conducts EEO, diversity, and regulatory review, ensures salary transparency standards, and gives final sign-off unlocking multi-portal publishing.

Job Description (JD) UI Role Mapping

1. Recruiter / HR View: Selects mandatory linked requisition, creates JDs using AI/Templates, edits content, and manages multi-channel distribution.
Stage: JD Author & Sourcing
2. Hiring Manager View: Inspects drafted skills, calibrates interview expectations, and approves technical scope for the team.
Stage: Technical Approval
3. HR / Compliance View: Performs legal, brand, and inclusivity verification; authorizes the JD for live job board syndication.
Stage: Compliance Approval
4. Super Admin View: Maintains enterprise role templates, automated workflow rules, and platform audit compliance.
Stage: Global Administration
2.3

Step-by-Step Job Description (JD) Flow

1
Recruiter Selects Mandatory Requisition & Drafts JD Actor: Recruiter

Recruiter opens JD Creator. Selects approved requisition from the mandatory dropdown (-- Select Linked Requisition -- ➔ REQ-2026-0001). Data (Title, Dept, Band, Openings) is auto-populated. Generates content via AI, Templates, or Manual drafting.

HR verifies compliance standards and clicks Approve Stage (HR Lead). JD status becomes APPROVED.

5
Recruiter Publishes Job to Multi-Portals Live Sourcing

Recruiter clicks Publish Job. Job posting is simultaneously syndicated to Careers Portal, LinkedIn, Indeed, Naukri, and Employee Referrals. Direct application flow activates!

Module Specification • Chapter 3

Job Posting & Multi-Channel Syndication (Distribution Governance)

End-to-end multi-board distribution, candidate source attribution, custom application screening, and campaign analytics.

3.1 Business Objective & Core Governance Rules

The Job Posting Hub acts as the enterprise broadcast center. Once a Job Description completes its mandatory 2-stage approval, recruiters can syndicate the approved job specifications across internal and external talent channels simultaneously with automated candidate tracking and screening.

🔒 Mandatory Approved JD

Strict governance block: Only JDs in Approved or Published state can be broadcast. Unapproved or drafted JDs are locked.

🎯 Automated UTM Source Attribution

Every channel receives a cryptographically generated, unique tracking link with parameters (utm_source=linkedin) to attribute 100% of candidate applications.

⚡ 1-Click Instant / Scheduled Broadcast

Publish immediately to all selected boards or schedule release dates with automated campaign expiry timers and budget caps.

The Resume Intake & Screening Engine unifies inbound applications across all broadcast portals into a consolidated, normalized talent pool linked directly to the Active Requisition. ⚡ Zero-Latency Inbound Sync

4.4 AI Parsing Engine & Semantic Match Scoring

Every ingested resume is processed by the PeopleWorks AI Parser, converting raw text into normalized entity vectors and generating a 0–100% Fit Score against the linked approved JD:

Weight: 40%
Technical Skills Match

Direct and semantic overlap with mandatory & preferred skills defined in JD.

Weight: 30%
Experience & Seniority

Total relevant years of experience compared to target band range (e.g., 5-8 yrs).

Weight: 15%
Education & Credentials

Verification of required degree (B.Tech/MCA), certifications, and academic pedigree.

Weight: 15%
Knockout & Notice Period

Availability within target notice period (≤ 30 days) and location/hybrid alignment.

4.5 Intelligent Deduplication Protocol & Conflict Matrix

To prevent duplicate profile creation when candidates apply via multiple portals, the system executes a 3-tier validation sequence:

1. Exact Email Match

If email matches existing database record within past 180 days, system triggers Duplicate Alert with previous application history.

2. Normalized Phone Match

E.164 normalized phone comparison identifies returning candidates even if alternate email address was utilized.

3. Resolution Options

Recruiter can: Merge Application, Create Multi-REQ Branch, or Discard Stale Submission.

4.6 Step-by-Step Candidate Intake & Screening Flow (Detailed Operational Walkthrough)

1
Multi-Source Ingestion & Active REQ Pipeline Sync Actor: Ingestion Engine / Recruiter

Candidate applications flow into the centralized intake queue through two primary pathways:

🌐 Automated Multi-Channel Sync

Candidates applying via Careers Portal, LinkedIn Easy Apply, Naukri, Indeed, or Employee Referral links arrive instantly with origin metadata (source=linkedin).

📁 Direct Recruiter Bulk Upload

Recruiters use Add / Upload Resume to drag & drop single PDF files or bulk ZIP archives (up to 50 resumes) bound directly to the active requisition (REQ-2026-0001).

UI Screen Outcome: Applications appear in candidate grid tagged with source badges and initial status NEW_APPLIED.
2
High-Speed OCR & Deep AI Entity Extraction Actor: AI Parser Engine

The optical character recognition (OCR) worker extracts raw document text and transforms unstructured resume files into structured talent vectors in < 2 seconds per file.

Extracted Entities & Data Fields:
• Contact: Name, Email, Phone
• Work Exp: 5.5 yrs total, Current role
• Skills: React, TypeScript, Redux
• Pedigree: B.Tech/MCA, Certifications
UI Screen Outcome: Zero manual typing needed. Candidate profile drawer auto-populates complete work timeline and verified skill tags.
3
3-Tier Deduplication & Knockout Evaluation Actor: System Validation

System validates incoming profile across 3 distinct data points to prevent duplicate talent records and verifies mandatory knockout requirements:

🔍 Deduplication Validation

Cross-checks Email, E.164 Phone, and LinkedIn URL against 180-day applicant history. If a match is found, system triggers a Duplicate Profile Alert with Merge/Branch options.

⚡ Knockout Criteria Evaluation

Compares applicant parameters with mandatory requirements (Notice ≤ 30 Days, Location: Bangalore). Failing candidates are tagged with Knockout Failed.

UI Screen Outcome: Candidates passing knockouts display green compliance indicators; non-compliant candidates display amber review flags.
4
Recruiter Screening & AI Fit Score Review Actor: Recruiter / Resume Manager

The Recruiter opens the candidate detail drawer to inspect the deep AI evaluation and conduct recruiter qualification:

AI Fit Score Algorithm Breakdown (0–100%):
• Skills Match (40%): JD tech stack overlap
• Experience (30%): Relevant years in band
• Education (15%): Required qualifications
• Knockout (15%): Notice period & shift fit
UI Action & Outcome: Recruiter confirms compensation expectation and clicks Mark as Shortlisted. Status advances to SHORTLISTED.
5
Hiring Manager Technical Sign-Off & Interview Handoff Actor: Hiring Manager / Reviewer

The Hiring Manager reviews the shortlisted talent queue to conduct technical qualification before interview scheduling:

👍 Technical Fit Feedback

HM examines code samples, past enterprise projects, and logs a Thumbs Up / Down rating with private notes (e.g., "Strong architecture background, recommend 1st round").

📅 1-Click Interview Initiation

HM clicks Schedule Technical Interview. The candidate record is automatically promoted to Module 5: Interview Scheduling & Evaluation with full audit history.

Next Pipeline Stage: Candidate enters Round 1 Technical Assessment queue with automated panelist calendar invites!

MODULE 5 Candidate 360 & Application Lifecycle

Candidate Management & Repository Module

Unified single source of truth for talent profiles across the enterprise. Features Candidate 360 profile decoupling (Person Entity vs Multi-Requisition Applications), 4-role deterministic RBAC, 6-stage recruitment pipeline, and cross-module live sync.

5.1 Candidate 360 Architecture: Person Entity vs. Applications

The Candidate Module decouples the core candidate identity (Person Entity) from requisition-specific applications (Application Entity). This allows a single candidate to maintain multiple concurrent or historical applications while preserving a consolidated audit record.

Candidate Entity (Person)
  • Primary ID (CAN-2026-XXXX)
  • Full Name, Primary Email, Verified Phone (E.164)
  • Current Location, Total Experience, Education Credentials
  • Master Skill Graph & Verified Skill Badges
  • SHA-256 Resume Hash & Non-Duplicate Certification
Application Entity (Job Specific)
  • Application ID (APP-2026-XXXX) linked to REQ-2026-XXXX
  • Target Job Title & Department
  • Current Stage (Interview) & Status (Active)
  • AI Fit Score against Requisition JD Criteria
  • Interviewer Scorecards, Notes & Offer Packages

5.3

Step-by-Step Candidate & Application Flow

1
Recruiter Ingests Candidate & System Deduplicates Actor: Recruiter / Ingestion Engine

Recruiter imports candidate profile from Resume Intake or creates record manually. System computes SHA-256 fingerprint hash and checks 180-day applicant history for duplicate collisions across Email and Phone. Candidate record initialized as APPLIED (CAN-2026-0089).

2
AI Evaluates Semantic Match & Recruiter Qualifies Profile Actor: AI Engine & Recruiter

AI benchmarks extracted skills against active Requisition JD, generating weighted score (e.g. 85% Match). Recruiter logs screening notes, confirms candidate communication proficiency, salary expectation, and notice period (≤ 30 Days). State becomes SCREENING.

3
Recruiter Marks Profile as Shortlisted for Technical Review Actor: Recruiter

Recruiter clicks Mark as Shortlisted. Action button transforms into an idempotent Shortlisted badge to prevent re-clicks. Candidate profile is automatically assigned to the Hiring Manager's requisition queue under SHORTLISTED.

4
Hiring Manager Reviews Technical Scope & Schedules Round 1 Actor: Hiring Manager

Hiring Manager opens Candidate 360 profile, inspects technical skills and project history, and clicks Schedule Technical Interview (R1). Candidate automatically progresses to Module 6 (Interviews Hub) in INTERVIEW stage with panel calendar invites.

5
Panel Conducts Technical Assessments & Submits Scorecards Actor: Interview Panel

Interview panel conducts Round 1 (Technical Automation), Round 2 (System Architecture), and HR Culture assessments. Structured scorecards and ratings are recorded. Cumulative panel recommendation triggers OFFER stage.

6
HR Admin Extends Approved Offer & Completes Hired Onboarding Actor: HR Admin / Compliance

HR Admin verifies compensation budget compliance (₹18L–₹24L), generates digitally-signed offer letter, and extends to candidate. Upon candidate acceptance, application status transitions to HIRED with automated handoff to HRIS and Pre-boarding!

5.4 The 6-Stage Recruitment Pipeline Specification

STAGE 1 Order: 1
Applied

Candidate application ingested via career portal, job board sync, employee referral, or resume upload. SHA-256 fingerprint deduplication verified.

STAGE 2 Order: 2
Screening

AI semantic engine parses resume skills and benchmarks candidate against active Requisition JD, generating weighted score (0–100%). Recruiter reviews fit.

STAGE 3 Order: 3
Shortlisted

Recruiter endorses profile. Candidate enters Hiring Manager's technical review queue for project fit and technical assessment greenlight.

STAGE 4 Order: 4
Interview

Technical assessments conducted across multiple rounds (Round 1 Tech, Round 2 System Design, HR Culture Fit). Feedback and ratings recorded.

STAGE 5 Order: 5
Offer

Compensation package approved by HOD/Finance. Official offer letter generated, digitally signed, and extended to candidate.

STAGE 6 Order: 6
Hired

Signed offer letter received. Candidate profile locked and automatically exported to HRIS and Pre-boarding module.
